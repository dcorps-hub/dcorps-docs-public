> Scope: Public overview of the entities and roles around dCorps, and how they are intended to relate.

Status note: dCorps is founder-led in Phase 0A (development). Unless explicitly stated otherwise, items below describe intended structure and are not executed legal arrangements.

---

## Bootstrap and foundation path (current intended path)

- dCorps Development Ltd. is incorporated and active in the British Virgin Islands (BVI) as the bootstrap development company.
- DevCo may build and operate early product surfaces, including controlled mainnet beta operations, while the foundation path is prepared.
- The standalone ResCo draft is retained only as a historical planning artifact unless later formalized.
- Public structure pages should treat the future foundation as the intended public-goods steward before public DCHUB distribution, liquidity, treasury activation, or token-governed authority.

---

## dCorps Foundation (planned; not yet incorporated)

- Intended role: neutral nonprofit steward of public goods (standards, conformance, audits coordination, ecosystem programs).
- Intended boundary: stewardship and coordination, not legal authority, and not automatic legal personhood for users.
- Current leading jurisdiction path is Abu Dhabi / ADGM.
- Incorporation details, board structure, and policies will be published when finalized.

See: [Foundation Policy](/policy/POL-FOUNDATION) and [Governance Transition Plan](/policy/POL-GOV-TRANSITION).

---

## DevCo and engineering providers

- dCorps Development Ltd. is the active BVI DevCo during bootstrap.
- Engineering work may also be performed by other service providers under contract.
- A Wyoming LLC may be used as an engineering services firm.
- That services role does not by itself imply stewardship, token custody, or protocol governance authority.
