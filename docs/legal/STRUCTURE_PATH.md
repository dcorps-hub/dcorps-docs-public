> Scope: Summary of the intended legal structure path described in the Whitepaper Long and governance transition policy.

---

## 1. Current posture (early phase)

Status note: dCorps is in Phase 0A (development). Mainnet is not live, the foundation is not incorporated yet, and dCorps Development Ltd. is the active BVI development company.

Current posture (design intention):

- The working legal path is **BVI DevCo bootstrap, then foundation before public DCHUB power**.
- **dCorps Development Ltd.** is incorporated and active in the **British Virgin Islands (BVI)**.
- DevCo may build and operate early product surfaces, contract with providers, receive service/software revenue, and hold or administer early IP/brand assets until the foundation path is executed.
- The target long-term steward remains a nonprofit foundation, with **Abu Dhabi / ADGM** as a leading candidate.
- A Wyoming LLC or other provider may be used as an engineering contractor where appropriate, but any such role is intended to remain a vendor / services role rather than protocol stewardship.
- This structure is not intended to create a custody or financial intermediation role.

---

## 2. Bootstrap and mainnet beta posture

- DevCo may operate a controlled mainnet beta before foundation incorporation if public claims remain accurate and conservative.
- Mainnet beta is not the same as public DCHUB Genesis/TGE.
- DCHUB gas during bootstrap may be sponsored, supplied, or otherwise controlled through the product and DevCo operating flows. Businesses should not need to buy public DCHUB from a market before Genesis/TGE.
- DevCo should not present itself as a bank, exchange, broker, custodian, public token issuer, foundation, or decentralized governance body.
- Public exchange, custody, on-ramp, public sale, or liquidity activity for DCHUB should not be launched without specific legal review and the foundation/steward gate described below.

---

## 3. Foundation and Genesis/TGE gate

- Establish a nonprofit foundation, or an equivalent steward arrangement, before public DCHUB distribution, public DCHUB liquidity, protocol treasury/community allocation activation, or live token-governed protocol authority.
- Put protocol and brand IP under the foundation through assignment, license, or another documented stewardship arrangement before public DCHUB power is activated.
- Use the foundation as the public-goods steward and off-chain coordinating entity once DCHUB becomes broadly distributed, liquid, treasury-active, or used for protocol governance.
- Allow the foundation to contract DevCo and other engineering providers under disclosed service agreements.
- Keep protocol neutrality and reduce single-vendor dependence.

---

## 4. Triggers and readiness signals

- Public testnet artifacts and reproducible tooling published.
- Governance processes operational with clear thresholds.
- Ecosystem contributors beyond the founding team are active.
- Mainnet beta controls disclosed if production operation starts before Genesis/TGE.
- Foundation incorporation, charter, and IP stewardship documents finalized before public DCHUB distribution, public liquidity, protocol treasury/community activation, or token-governed authority.

---

## 5. Change control

Any material change to the legal structure path should be reflected in updates to this document and, once on-chain governance is live, documented via governance proposals where applicable.
