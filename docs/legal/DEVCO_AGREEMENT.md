> Scope: Public summary of the active DevCo bootstrap role and its intended relationship to the future foundation.

Status note: This is not an executed contract and does not constitute legal advice. dCorps Development Ltd. is the active BVI development company. The foundation is planned and not incorporated yet.

---

## 1. Active DevCo role

- **dCorps Development Ltd.** is incorporated and active in the **British Virgin Islands (BVI)**.
- DevCo may build and operate early product surfaces, maintain reference interfaces, contract with providers, and receive service/software revenue.
- DevCo may hold or administer early IP and brand assets until the foundation or another steward arrangement is ready.
- DevCo may supply or sponsor DCHUB gas through controlled product flows during bootstrap.
- DevCo is not the foundation, not a bank, not an exchange, not a broker, not a custodian, and not a decentralized governance body.

---

## 2. Foundation relationship

- The foundation remains the intended long-term public-goods steward.
- The foundation, once formed and ready, may receive IP stewardship through assignment, license, or another documented legal arrangement.
- The foundation may contract DevCo for defined engineering or operational milestones, but that relationship should be disclosed, scoped, and reviewable.
- Public DCHUB distribution, public liquidity, protocol treasury/community allocation activation, and live token-governed authority should not proceed until the foundation or an equivalent steward path is executed and publicly disclosed.

---

## 3. Mainnet beta boundary

- A controlled mainnet beta may operate before the foundation exists.
- Mainnet beta should not be presented as public DCHUB Genesis/TGE.
- Any DCHUB used for gas in that stage should be sponsored, supplied, or otherwise controlled through the product and DevCo operating flows.
- Public sale, public liquidity, custody, exchange, or on-ramp activity for DCHUB requires separate legal review and the steward gate described in [docs/legal/STRUCTURE_PATH.md](/legal/STRUCTURE_PATH).

---

## 4. Change control

Any material change to DevCo’s role should be reflected in this document, [docs/legal/STRUCTURE_PATH.md](/legal/STRUCTURE_PATH), and the relevant governance transition policy.
