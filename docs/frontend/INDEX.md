## Current state

- The canonical public surface is now `dcorps-app`.
- Public docs remain canonical for specs, policies, and whitepaper material.
- Legacy static website repos may remain as transition artifacts, but they are no longer the center of the public product experience.

---

## Target surfaces

- Official dCorps App (entity management console).
- Registry and explorer basics inside the app during the current phase.
- Dashboards and reporting views derived from the indexer.
- Wallet-connected workflows.
